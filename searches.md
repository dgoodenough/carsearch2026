# Pre-filtered searches — San Diego

Every link below was checked against the live site. The CarGurus links in the
first version of this file were wrong — they used model IDs I had guessed rather
than looked up, and one of them landed on Mercedes convertibles. These are the
real ones.

**What each site actually honours.** CarGurus applies price and mileage from the
URL but ignores a year filter, so set the years in its sidebar. Autotrader applies
price and radius. Cars.com and CarMax take the full set. Craigslist takes price,
year and clean-title. No site can exclude a colour — only include one — so red
gets screened by eye, or by the finder.

Sorted cheapest-first where the site supports it.

## The instant-offer check — do this before you buy, not after

Uncle Dave's advice here is the most valuable thing in this whole file. Before
committing to any car, put its **year, mileage and trim** into the sell-side
tools and write down what they'd pay for it:

- [CarMax — get an offer](https://www.carmax.com/sell-my-car)
- [Carvana — get an offer](https://www.carvana.com/sell-my-car)
- [KBB instant cash offer](https://www.kbb.com/instant-cash-offer/)
- [Edmunds appraisal](https://www.edmunds.com/appraisal/)

That number, minus what you paid, minus about $1,200 in California tax and
registration, is your real cost of the car. Add roughly 8,000 miles to the
odometer before you enter it — that's what she'll drive in eight months, and it
is what the car will actually have on it when you sell.

## Before you hand anyone money

**Call your insurer first.** The dealer will not let the car off the lot without
proof of California coverage, and this is the step that strands people on a
Friday afternoon. Confirm your carrier writes in California and get the quote
before the 24th.

**Get a pre-purchase inspection** — $100–200 mobile in San Diego, and the highest
return on any money you spend this trip. On any Prius, ask specifically for
hybrid battery block voltages; a pack about to fail is invisible on a test drive
and is a $2–3k surprise.

**California requires the seller to provide a valid smog certificate** issued
within 90 days — dealers and private sellers both. No certificate, no
registration. Do not accept "you can get it done after."

**On a Gen 3 Prius (2010–2015), walk away** unless a shop has already documented
the EGR circuit and head gasket. That is the cheap-Prius trap.
